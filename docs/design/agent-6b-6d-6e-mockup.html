6bAgent awareness

Rows show the slash command you ran, and running / needs you / done. Double-click a row's title to rename it. Ctrl+Shift+J or "Next waiting" jumps to what needs you.

6dFloating panes + scratch shell

Click a server in the bottom section: it floats over your session (drag the header, resize the corner, × or Expand). Ctrl+Shift+` or ⌨ toggles a scratch shell. Panes belong to their session.

6ePalette + Commands page

Ctrl+Shift+K or the search bar: what's waiting, sessions, your skills and kaze's own actions. Settings gets a read-only Commands page.
