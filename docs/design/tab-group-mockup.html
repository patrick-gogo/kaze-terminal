Tab groups (temporary, before real panes)

A workspace's tabs live inside one group in the tab strip, so samurai takes the space of one tab. Two ways it could look. Click the group, pick a tab; press A in variant A to simulate "storefront needs you".

A. Collapsed chip with a dropdown (recommended)

B. Expands in place when you're in it

When you are on a samurai tab, its members show as small tabs inside the group. Switch to another tab and the group shrinks back to one chip. Faster to switch inside the group, but it uses more room while you're in it.
