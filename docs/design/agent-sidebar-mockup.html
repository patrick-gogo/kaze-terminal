Clickable mockup. Click sessions and group headers. Ctrl+K (or the search bar) opens the palette. Ctrl+J (or "Next waiting") jumps to a session that needs you. Ctrl+B (or ☰) hides the sidebar. Click the chips in the bottom bar. Servers live at the bottom of the sidebar: click one to open it as a floating pane (drag the header, resize from the bottom-right corner, Esc hides the top one). Ctrl+` (or the ⌨ button) toggles a scratch shell. Floating panes belong to their session: switch sessions and come back. Ticket keys, counts and output are made up.
